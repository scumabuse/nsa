# Space Apps submission preparation

Use the team’s own observations and wording for the official Project Page. This guide maps evidence in the prototype to the supplied judging criteria and records the handoff work.

| Criterion | Evidence to show | What the team can verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impact | Onboard logging and a readable handoff while disconnected | Time a crew member adding a reading, finding the change and downloading the handoff |
| Creativity | The training scenario connects an observed change to a trend, a next step and a handoff | Run the scenario from a fresh browser profile |
| Scientific and technical validity | Personal baselines, meal-context matching, PDS dose-rate data with direct provenance | Compare two observations with their PDS source and explain why rover readings cannot estimate crew exposure |
| Relevance | Six health metrics, measurement guidance and the five spaceflight hazards | Show the symptom escalation instruction and how a routine reading differs |
| Presentation | English working prototype, actual screenshots and a short workflow | Rehearse the pitch and verify links from a signed-out browser |

## Project Page

- **High-Level Summary.** In two or three sentences, describe the onboard problem your team chose and the workflow it implemented.
- **How we addressed the challenge.** Explain the team’s design decisions, what you tested and what you would validate before a mission.
- **Project Demo.** Add a link the judges can open. The current Sites release is private.
- **Data & Resources Usage.** Cite the specific PDS product, label, instrument specification and NASA HRP resource in the [README](README.md). Disclose that Codex developed and tested the starting prototype and this revision. Add the team’s own contributions and any other tools used.
- **Source Code.** Publish the source with its README and license under the team’s account and verify access while signed out.

## Pitch format

Choose a video up to 30 seconds or a presentation up to seven slides. For a short video, open directly on the sleep scenario, show the handoff download and then switch to the live archive chart. For slides, allocate space to the problem, screenshots of the crew workflow, NASA data and its limits, test results, and team contributions. Use a public link and verify it while signed out.

## Eligibility check

Review the current guidance in the team dashboard and with your Local Lead. The supplied briefing says that fully AI-generated submissions are ineligible. Because Codex implemented the prototype, the team should establish its own substantive contribution and confirm eligibility before submitting. Keep the official Project Page and pitch in English as specified in the supplied briefing.

The [official 2026 calendar](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) lists the submission deadline as November 15 at 11:59 p.m. local time. See the [official terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/) for licensing, citations and prohibited submission content.
